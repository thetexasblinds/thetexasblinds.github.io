Questions

Good to know

Do you sell the blinds?

No. We install blinds you've already bought from a store, an online retailer or a dealer. If you haven't ordered yet, we can measure for $75 so you order the right sizes.

What if my blinds don't fit?

Size and fit are the responsibility of whoever measured. Our work covers the mounting and hardware. If we take the measurements, we stand behind them.

How long does an install take?

Most standard windows take 15–30 minutes each. A typical whole house is done in a few hours.

Do you install motorized blinds?

Yes. Send us the brand and window count and we'll quote it.

How do I pay?

Cash, card, Zelle or check. Apartments and trade accounts can be invoiced Net 15.
